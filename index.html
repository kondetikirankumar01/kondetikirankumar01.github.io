COMPETE FAIR

Tournament Rules

All players and teams must follow these rules. Tournament-specific rules may add requirements and take priority where applicable.

  1. Accurate Details — Submit correct team, player, IGN, and UID details.
  2. Registered Players Only — Only registered players may participate.
  3. Fair Play — No hacks, cheats, exploits, scripts, or unauthorized software.
  4. No Teaming — Teaming with opponents is strictly prohibited.
  5. No Account Sharing — Players must use their own registered accounts.
  6. Match Timings — Join matches on time and follow all room instructions.
  7. Follow Tournament Rules — Follow all tournament-specific rules and organizer instructions.
  8. No Misconduct — Harassment, abuse, disruption, or result manipulation is prohibited.
  9. Evidence — Organizers may request screenshots, recordings, or other proof for disputes.
  10. Disqualification — Rule violations may result in penalties or immediate disqualification.
  11. Organizer Decision — Organizer decisions regarding disputes and penalties are final.
  12. Tournament-Specific Rules — Additional rules for a tournament take priority where applicable.

PAYMENT & REFUNDS

Refund Policy

Our refund policy for tournament entries and wallet transactions.

1. Tournament Entry Refunds

Refunds may be issued in the following cases:

  • Tournament Cancellation: If a tournament is cancelled by Lock & Load Club, all registered participants will be eligible for a refund.
  • Technical Issues: If a platform-side technical issue, such as a lobby failure or major match disruption, prevents the tournament from proceeding, a refund may be provided after verification.

Refund requests must be submitted within 14 days of the cancellation or technical issue. Late requests may not be considered.

2. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be provided for:

  • Missing a match due to personal reasons or lack of preparation.
  • Disqualification or bans due to cheating, misconduct, or rule violations.
  • Removal from a tournament for breaking tournament rules or terms.

3. Wallet Deposits

Wallet top-ups are non-refundable once credited to your account, except for verified payment issues.

For failed or duplicate transactions, a refund may be considered if:

  • The request is submitted within 14 days of the transaction.
  • Valid proof, such as a payment screenshot or transaction ID, is provided.

Requests without sufficient proof may be rejected.
